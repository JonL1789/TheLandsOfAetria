One of the four main player characters. 

As a child, Four had a ritual performed upon them in order to transfuse their being into a Lycan - Human Hybrid. This unique blood, when transfused with 



Ballistophobia and Hybrid Transformation - Incomplete Form are now replaced with: 

Undying Will to Understand Oneself
=
Four may now exchange forms with Five at any time, (If performed in battle, can only be done once per turn.) While in the form of Four, gain the following abilities:

- Unforgiving Mercy
	- Cost: One Action, Once per Long Rest
	- Brandish your blood-tinged greatsword, and plunge it into the ground below you. Utilizing the arcana within your unique hybrid blood, construct a way back into the fight when the darkness seems to cast away all hope. A gentle blood-red radiance emanates from your body, as you will cast away 3d10 of your Current HP. 
	- For three turns, grant up to three allies in a 10 feet radius the follow buffs.
		- **Sanguine Garden of Familial Love**
		    - Against a single target, you heal 10% (rounded up) of outgoing attack damage as Temp HP on a damaging hit.
		    - +2 to Hit, +2 to Damage
		    - +1 AC
    

While in the form of Five, gain the following effects:

- Unforgiving Justice: _Active_
    
    - You may cast away 2d8 of your max HP (rounded up) to do an additional 3d8 additional damage of the attack’s type on a single action, (once per turn.)
    - This max HP reduction cannot be healed in any way, and only is regained at a long rest.
    

**TEMPORARY PASSIVE:** Dormant Light of Family, Reawakened

- Embody the forgotten light within your claws, and shine back the darkness that once bound you taught in despair. You must confront this evil; if not for you, then let it be for the people you call family.
    
    - Heal 50% of your max HP.
    - Once per turn, Embolden your bloodied claws with a searing radiant flame, converting any and all damage performed in one action into **SoulFire** damage.
	    - SoulFire Damage: Any and all damage inflicted through ***SoulFire*** damage will always hit and will always critically hit.
