The emotion of hopelessness, concentrated into a liquid form. Consuming this liquid commonly afflicts the user with [[Violet Rot]].

There are three stages of this:
Violet Rot has Three Stages: 
Stage One: Weakened emotions, slight discolor to skin.

Stage Two: Apathetic and emotionless. Victims cough violet colored blood occasionally, and have compulsive tendencies. Speaks about a safe feeling from the Violet Rot. Enters a more primal state of mind, yet a thread tied to a sense of Humanity remains. Eyes are tinged purple. 

Stage Three: Foregone of human connection. Extremely violent to those near. Purple veins have engorged their skin. Territorial, fight or flight mode on constantly.
